# HIG review: M4 playback

Result: **PASS** for the screenshot pass, with two fixes made during the milestone gate (the 360px overflow and the action bar's style). The milestone stays open for the user's review and the accessibility walk-throughs listed at the end, which need a person with Orca and a keyboard.

Files: `app/` (`playback.rs`, `controls.rs`, `media.rs`, `actions.rs`, `window.rs`), `player/` (`mod.rs`, `mpris.rs`), `ui/player_bar/`, `ui/details_pane/`, `ui/show_hero.rs`, `ui/show_page.rs`, `ui/new_view.rs`, `ui/content/`, `ui/selection.rs`, `ui/selection_bar.rs`, `ui/widgets/episode_row/`, `ui/links.rs`, `ui/dialogs/description.rs`, `data/style.css`, the new symbolic icons.

Pages checked (fetched 2026-11-03 and 2026-11-04): buttons, boxed lists, selection and edit modes, writing style, UI icons, pointer and touch, keyboard and the standard keyboard shortcuts, sliders, header bars, at https://developer.gnome.org/hig/…. The quotes behind each decision are in `docs/decisions.md`.

## Findings

1. **[PASS] One suggested button per view.** Buttons: "Each view should only ever include a single button using either the suggested or destructive styles." The details pane's Play is its one pill. The requirements also make a podcast page's Subscribe the accent button; in wide windows both can show at once, recorded when the pane was built.
2. **[PASS] Circular and flat buttons where small buttons sit together.** Buttons: circular buttons "can be useful in situations where a number of smaller buttons are positioned in close proximity". The player bar's transport and the details pane's toggles.
3. **[PASS] Icon-only buttons are labelled.** Every icon button has a tooltip and an accessible label that says what it does (Add to or Remove From Queue, and so on), checked in the code and the template.
4. **[PASS] Custom icons are symbolic.** UI Icons: "If you require an icon that doesn't already exist, it is possible to create your own." The queue, played, download and skip icons are 16px symbolic SVGs, crisp at scale 2.
5. **[PASS] Chapters are a boxed list.** Boxed Lists: "appropriate for relatively small static lists". The current chapter is grey and bold, in light, dark and high contrast.
6. **[PASS] Selection mode as the HIG lays it out, with one departure.** Selection & Edit Modes: three or more actions (four); secondary click and long press select; "When one or more items are selected, selection mode should be active"; a Cancel button and the count in the header; an action bar at the bottom. The check box does not show on hover, as the page asks ("In normal mode, a check box should be shown over each content item on hover"): the row menu's Select replaces it, decided by the user on 2026-11-04.
7. **[PASS] Standard shortcuts.** Ctrl+A selects all and Shift+Ctrl+A none, as in the standard keyboard shortcuts; Escape leaves selection mode.
8. **[FIXED] The action bar did not stand apart from the list.** It now has the toolbar's raised style, seen in light, dark and high contrast.
9. **[FIXED] New and the player bar were wider than a 360px window.** A partly played row's progress bar kept Adwaita's 150px trough minimum, and the seek bar asked for 240px beside its times, so the narrow player bar needed 388px even with nothing playing. Both now fit (`ui/display_tests.rs`); New's margins are even at 360×640 in light and dark.
10. **[PASS] Header capitalization** for buttons, menu items, tooltips and headings ("About This Episode", "Mark as Unplayed", "Select All", "No Episodes Selected"); sentence style for status descriptions, no closing periods.
